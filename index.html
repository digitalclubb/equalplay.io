Why it's built this way

Your age grade decides what you see

Regulation 15 decides what your age group is allowed to do: tackling from U9, rucks and scrums from U10, lineouts from U12. So a U8 coach won't find a ruck drill in here however hard they look for one. It isn't a setting somebody can switch off by mistake. Every claim Equal Play makes about an age grade links out to the RFU's own wording as well, because theirs is the version that counts to a referee. If it's the minutes you're after rather than the drills, Regulation 15 playing time covers those. For what each grade may actually practise, from tag at U7 through the first tackles at U9 to lineouts at U12, there is a page per age group.

Nothing about a child is ever stored

Player names stay on your phone and never reach a server. There's no roster sitting in a database and no attendance register anywhere, because neither would be worth the risk of holding. If a parent asks you to take their child off, you delete a name on your own phone and that's the end of it. The privacy notice spells out the rest.

It works at the pitch with no signal

Open it at home the night before and it'll still be there when your phone has one bar and nothing behind it. Session plans get written to the phone the moment you type. They go up to the server afterwards, whenever a signal turns up, so a dead connection can't cost you the session you just spent half an hour putting together. The buttons are big for a reason, which is that you'll be tapping them with cold wet hands.

Who makes this

One volunteer coach, for the age group they were already coaching on a Sunday. There is no company behind it, no funding, nothing sold on. It gates drills by age grade because getting that wrong at a session is a safety matter rather than a preference.

  • 104 drills written from scratch rather than lifted from somewhere else
  • 30 ready-made sessions covering U7 through to U12
  • 6 age grades, each one linked to the RFU's own rules of play so you can check us
  • No ads, nothing sold, nothing stored about a child

Equal Play is not affiliated with the RFU. Regulation 15 is theirs. We link to it rather than reproduce it, so you always get the current version.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

Not to look round. Pick your age group and every drill for that grade is there to read, with no email address involved. Match day is the same, free for good. An account is for the things that have to still be there next week, which means the sessions you build and the drills you star.

Do you store anything about the children?

No. That's the one thing we won't bend on. Player names live on the phone in your pocket and never reach a server, which also means there's nothing for anybody to lose.

Is it rugby only?

The drills are, deliberately. They are written to RFU Regulation 15 for U7 to U12, which is the whole reason the age limits in here mean anything. Match day is the exception, because there's nothing rugby-specific in the fairness maths, so it works just as well for football or netball. If the arithmetic is all you want, the equal playing time calculator does that on its own.

How does the fairness actually work?

Everybody carries a debt, which is the game time they were owed for the games they were around for, minus what they've actually had. Whoever is owed the most goes on next. It works that way because a fixed rotation falls apart the moment one child turns up at half nine, whereas a debt just recalculates and carries on. The rugby substitution app page goes through it properly.
