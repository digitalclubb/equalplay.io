What an U11 grade is allowed to do

U11 plays the U10 game with a year more of it behind them. Tackle, ruck, maul and uncontested scrum are all in. The lineout still is not, so it stays out of everything on this page until U12.

The same shape as U10 with more of it. Still no lineout. The RFU sets this out in Regulation 15 rather than leaving it to a coach's judgement. The U11 rules of play are the version that counts to a referee, they are reissued every season, so check yours there rather than trusting a copy on somebody else's website. Ours included.

What to work on at U11

  • Ruck speed. They can all ruck by now. Doing it in two seconds rather than six is what changes a game.
  • Defending as a pair. Two players who talk to each other beat two players who are quicker and do not.
  • Using the whole pitch. An U11 squad will happily play the entire match inside ten metres unless somebody makes them stop.
  • Handling under a bit of pressure. Not cones. A defender, moving, close enough to be a nuisance.

Because no new law arrives, U11 is the grade where coaching actually shows. There is no scramble to introduce a skill before Christmas, so a season can be spent on decisions: when to pass, when to carry, when to keep it. Twenty-nine of these drills are game sense for that reason, which is more than any other grade carries.

Common questions

What is new at U11 rugby?

Nothing in the laws, which is the point. U11 is a consolidation year under Regulation 15 and the tackle, ruck and scrum are all carried over from U10. It is the one season where you are not teaching something brand new against the clock.

Can U11s have a lineout?

No. The lineout is introduced at U12. U11 restarts and free kicks are worth practising properly instead, because they are how the ball comes back into play all season.

What should U11 training focus on?

Speed and decisions rather than new skills. Quicker ball at the ruck, defenders who talk, an attack that uses the width of the pitch. The skills are already there by U11 so the work is making them happen faster and in the right place.
